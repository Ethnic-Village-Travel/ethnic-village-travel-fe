# Product Overview

Ethnic Village Travel is a tourism booking platform focused on ethnic minority cultural experiences in Vietnam. The
platform enables users to discover, book, and manage tours to ethnic villages, featuring authentic cultural experiences
with local communities (Hmong, Thai, Muong, etc.).

## Key Features

- **Tour Discovery**: Browse tours by ethnic group, location, duration, and price
- **Booking System**: Complete booking flow with guest information, payment integration, and order management
- **Multi-language Support**: Vietnamese and English localization
- **User Accounts**: Personal dashboard for bookings, bookmarks, and transaction history
- **Admin Dashboard**: Tour management, employee assignment, and booking oversight
- **Review System**: User reviews and ratings for tours
- **Promotion System**: Discount codes and promotional offers

## User Roles

- **Customers**: Browse and book tours, manage bookmarks, write reviews
- **Admins**: Manage tours, assign employees, oversee bookings and operations
- **Employees**: Tour guides and staff assigned to specific tour dates
